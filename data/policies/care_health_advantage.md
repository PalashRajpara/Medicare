# Care Health Advantage & Supreme Policy Guidelines

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
A waiting period of 30 days from policy commencement applies to all medical hospitalizations. Emergency hospitalizations caused by accidental injury are exempt from this waiting period.

## 2. Specified Illnesses Waiting Period
A waiting period of 24 consecutive months applies to named procedures and elective treatments:
- Joint replacement surgeries (Knee, Hip, Shoulder).
- Benign tumors, cysts, polyps, and sinus corrections.
- Cataract surgery (sub-limit of ₹30,000 to ₹50,000 per eye depending on Sum Insured).
- Hernia repairs, hemorrhoids, fistulae, and fissure treatments.
- Gallstones and kidney stone removal surgeries.

## 3. Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) Waiting Period
Declared pre-existing medical conditions (hypertension, diabetes, thyroid disorders, asthma) carry a 36-month waiting period. An optional Care PED Modification rider reduces this waiting period to 24 or 12 months upon additional premium.

## 4. Cashless Pre-Authorization Protocol
- Pre-authorization forms must be submitted to the hospital TPA desk 48 hours in advance for planned surgeries.
- For emergency admissions, intimation must be provided within 24 hours of hospital entry.
- Required documents include doctor's advice note, diagnostic tests justifying admission, and government ID card.

## 5. Room Rent Entitlement
- For Sum Insured < ₹5 Lakhs: 1% of Sum Insured per day.
- For Sum Insured ≥ ₹5 Lakhs: Single Private Room eligibility with no proportionate deductions applied to ancillary procedural billing.

## 6. Robotic Surgeries and Advance Medical Care
Covers advanced technological medical interventions such as Robotic Surgery, High-Field Intraoperative MRI, Laser Interventions, and Balloon Sinuplasty up to specified sub-limits or base Sum Insured.

## 7. Claim Settlement and Grievance Escalation
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days from date of discharge. If a claim is repudiated, an appeal can be lodged with the Care Health Grievance Redressal Committee within 30 days. Escalation is available to the Insurance Ombudsman if unresolved after 30 days.

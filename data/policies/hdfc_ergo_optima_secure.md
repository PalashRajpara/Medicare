# HDFC ERGO Optima Secure Policy Guidelines

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
All medical claims are subject to an initial waiting period of 30 days from the inception date of the policy. Accidental bodily injuries requiring emergency in-patient hospitalization are covered immediately from day one without any waiting period.

## 2. Specified Illnesses & Joint Replacement Waiting Period
A waiting period of 24 months of continuous coverage applies for designated surgeries and degenerative conditions:
- Total knee replacement, total hip replacement, and degenerative osteoarthritis surgeries.
- Gallbladder removal (Cholecystectomy) and urinary tract stone procedures (URS, PCNL, RIRS).
- Cataract surgery (subject to specific Sum Insured sub-limits).
- Internal tumors, cysts, polyps, and sinus surgeries unless biopsied as malignant.

## 3. Pre-Existing Conditions Waiting Period
Pre-existing diseases declared at policy inception are eligible for full claim settlement after 36 months of continuous renewals. Under Optima Secure, no co-payment applies once the waiting period has elapsed.

## 4. Cashless Network Empanelment & Protocol
- Cashless facility is available across more than 12,000 network partner hospitals nationwide.
- **Submission Window**: Pre-authorization requests must be initiated at least 48 hours prior to planned hospital admission, and within 24 hours for emergency admission.
- Decisions on cashless pre-authorization guarantees are communicated to the hospital TPA within 2 hours of receiving complete documentation.

## 5. Room Rent Eligibility (Zero Proportionate Deductions)
Under HDFC ERGO Optima Secure, there is **no room rent capping or sub-limit** across all sum insured tiers. The insured patient is eligible for up to a Single Private Standard Air-Conditioned room without incurring any proportionate reduction penalties on doctor fees, nursing charges, or surgical billing.

## 6. Modern Robotic and Specialized Treatments
Covers modern treatments without sub-limits up to the Sum Insured, including Robotic Surgeries, Oral Chemotherapy, Intra Vitreal Injections, and Immunotherapy. Organ donor in-patient hospitalization expenses are covered in full up to the base sum insured.

## 7. Claim Deficiency Response and Appeal Mechanism
If an initial cashless request is denied due to query deficiency or lack of clinical history:
1. The insured may settle the bill at discharge and submit a Reimbursement Claim within 15 days of discharge.
2. Provide original discharge summary, itemized hospital bill, payment receipts, diagnostic films/reports, and indoor case papers.
3. If aggrieved by a rejection decision, appeal directly to the HDFC ERGO Grievance Redressal Cell. Further escalation can be made to the Insurance Ombudsman under the Redressal of Public Grievances Rules.

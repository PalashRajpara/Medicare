# Niva Bupa ReAssure 2.0 Comprehensive Policy

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
An initial waiting period of 30 days applies from policy inception. Emergency accidental medical treatments are admissible immediately without a waiting period.

## 2. Specified Disease Waiting Period
A waiting period of 24 months applies for specified surgeries:
- Joint replacement (Total Knee Replacement, Hip Replacement).
- Cataract surgery (sub-limit according to sum insured tier).
- Hernia, piles, fissures, and gallbladder stones.
- Benign prostatic enlargement and non-infective arthritis.

## 3. Pre-Existing Conditions Waiting Period
Pre-existing diseases declared at policy issuance are covered after 36 months of continuous renewals. With the ReAssure+ rider, this waiting period may be reduced to 24 months.

## 4. Cashless Network Processing
- Over 9,500 network hospitals supported.
- Planned admission pre-authorization must be submitted 48 hours prior to hospitalization.
- Emergency hospitalization requests must be initiated within 24 hours of admission.
- Fast-track initial cashless decisions within 30 minutes at premier network hospitals.

## 5. Room Rent Eligibility (Lock the Clock Rider)
Under ReAssure 2.0, there is **no capping on room rent** (any room category up to single private room or suite depending on sum insured tier) with zero proportionate deductions on surgical or professional consultation bills.

## 6. Modern Technological Surgeries
Robotic surgeries, intra-operative neuro monitoring, and specialized laser surgeries are covered up to the base sum insured without restrictive sub-limits.

## 7. Claim Deficiency Resolution & Appeals
Reimbursement claims must be lodged within 30 days of hospital discharge. Rejections can be appealed within 30 days to the Niva Bupa Grievance Redressal Officer. Escalation can be directed to the IRDAI Ombudsman portal.

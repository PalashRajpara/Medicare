# Bajaj Allianz Health Guard Gold Policy

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
30 days from the inception date of the policy for all illnesses, excluding emergency admissions caused by accidental injuries.

## 2. Specific Illnesses Waiting Period
A waiting period of 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage applies for elective and designated conditions:
- Knee and hip replacement surgeries.
- Cataract surgery (up to ₹40,000 per eye).
- Gallbladder stones, renal calculus, and hernia repair.
- Hysterectomy, internal tumors, and joint degeneration.

## 3. Pre-Existing Diseases Waiting Period
Pre-existing ailments declared at policy inception require 36 months of continuous coverage before claim admissibility.

## 4. Cashless Pre-Authorization Protocol
- Empaneled network across 8,500+ healthcare providers in India.
- Pre-authorization forms must be submitted to the hospital TPA desk 48 hours before planned admission, or within 24 hours for emergency admission.
- Pre-auth approvals or deficiency queries are issued within 2 to 4 hours.

## 5. Room Rent and ICU Capping Rules
- For Sum Insured up to ₹5,00,000: Normal room rent is capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day. ICU rent is capped at 2% per day. Proportionate deductions apply to associated treatment fees if higher category rooms are selected.
- For Sum Insured of ₹7,50,000 and above: Single Private Room permitted with no proportionate deductions.

## 6. Robotic Surgeries and Modern Treatments
Robotic surgeries, stereotactic radio surgeries, and oral chemotherapy are covered up to 50% of the Sum Insured.

## 7. Claim Repudiation and Appeals
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of discharge. If a claim is rejected, an appeal can be filed with the Bajaj Allianz Grievance Redressal Officer. Further escalation is available through the Insurance Ombudsman.

# Star Health Comprehensive Insurance Policy

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
A standard waiting period of 30 days from the policy inception date applies to all illnesses and hospitalizations, except for emergency medical admissions resulting directly from accidental bodily injury.

## 2. Specific Illnesses and Surgical Waiting Periods
A mandatory waiting period of 24 consecutive months of uninterrupted coverage applies for specified diseases and scheduled treatments, including:
- Total and partial joint replacement surgeries (knee, hip, shoulder) unless necessitated by an acute accidental trauma.
- Cataract surgery with intraocular lens implantation (capped at ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per eye depending on Sum Insured).
- Benign prostatic hypertrophy (BPH), hydrocele, varicocele, and hernia repairs (inguinal, umbilical, incisional).
- Stones in the biliary and urinary systems (gallbladder stones, renal calculi, ureteric calculi).
- Hysterectomy for menorrhagia or fibromyoma, and prolapse of intervertebral disc (PID).

## 3. Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) Waiting Period
Pre-existing conditions, illnesses, or chronic medical conditions declared during proposal submission are covered after 36 months of continuous, break-free coverage. If optional PED buyback riders were selected, this waiting period may be reduced to 12 months.

## 4. Cashless Pre-Authorization Protocol
- **Planned Admissions**: Pre-authorization request forms must be submitted to the hospital Third Party Administrator (TPA) / Insurance Helpdesk at least 48 hours prior to scheduled admission.
- **Emergency Admissions**: The TPA or insurer must be notified within 24 hours of emergency hospitalization.
- **Documentation**: Provide the treating specialist's diagnostic prescription, proposed cost estimation letter on hospital letterhead, Star Health digital health card, and government photo identification (Aadhaar or Voter ID). Initial cashless query responses are rendered within 2 hours.

## 5. Room Rent and ICU Capping Rules
- For Sum Insured up to ₹5,00,000: Room rent is capped at 1% of the Sum Insured per day. ICU charges are capped at 2% of the Sum Insured per day. Proportionate deductions apply to associated medical charges if an upgraded room category is occupied.
- For Sum Insured of ₹10,00,000 and above: Single Private Standard Air-Conditioned Room is permitted without any proportionate deduction or room rent capping.

## 6. Modern Treatments and Daycare Procedures
Coverage is extended to advanced modern medical treatments up to 50% of Sum Insured (or policy sub-limits), including Robotic Surgeries, Stem Cell Therapy, Deep Brain Stimulation, Balloon Sinuplasty, and Stereotactic Radio Surgeries. Over 400 daycare procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization are admissible.

## 7. Exclusions and Non-Payable Charges
- Expenses incurred for non-medical consumables, gloves, syringes, administrative admission charges, and hygiene kits are non-payable.
- Treatment for alcohol/drug dependency, intentional self-inflicted injuries, and cosmetic enhancement surgeries are permanently excluded.

## 8. Claim Rejection & Grievance Escalation Procedure
In the event of a claim rejection (e.g., non-disclosure of pre-existing ailments or missing documentation):
1. Written appeal can be submitted within 30 days of the rejection memo to the Star Health Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) along with complete case indoor case sheets (IPD papers) and attending physician clarification.
2. If unresolved within 15 days, escalate to the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) Bima Bharosa portal or file a formal complaint with the jurisdictional Insurance Ombudsman.

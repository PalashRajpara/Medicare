# Cigna Global Health Options Guidelines

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
All medical claims for routine sickness are subject to an initial waiting period of 30 days from the policy effective date. Accidental bodily injuries requiring emergency in-patient admission or trauma stabilization are covered immediately from day one without any waiting period.

## 2. Specified Illnesses & Joint Replacement Waiting Period
A waiting period of 24 months of continuous policy coverage applies to major elective joint surgeries, joint degeneration, and specified chronic procedures:
- Total knee replacement, partial knee arthroplasty, and total hip replacement.
- Degenerative disc disease, spinal fusion, and laminectomy procedures.
- Non-emergency gallbladder surgery (cholecystectomy), kidney and urinary stone extractions (PCNL, URS).
- Elective hernia repairs, hemorrhoidectomy, and benign sinus endoscopic interventions.

## 3. Pre-Existing Conditions Waiting Period
Pre-existing medical conditions explicitly accepted during medical underwriting are eligible for benefits after a 24-month waiting period of continuous active coverage. Where standard underwriting with a moratorium applies, conditions must remain treatment-free, symptom-free, and advice-free for a consecutive 24-month window prior to claim eligibility.

## 4. Cashless Direct Billing & Pre-Authorization Protocol
- **Direct Settlement Network**: Cigna Global maintains direct settlement agreements across accredited premier medical centers in India, including Apollo Hospitals, Fortis Healthcare, Max Super Speciality, Manipal Hospitals, Narayana Health, and Medanta.
- **Guarantee of Payment (GOP)**: For planned elective procedures, the hospital or insured member must submit a pre-authorization request at least 72 hours prior to hospital admission. For emergency admissions, notice must be provided within 48 hours of admission.
- Once approved, Cigna issues a formal Guarantee of Payment (GOP) directly to the hospital billing desk, waiving cash deposits up to the approved limit.

## 5. Room Rent Eligibility (Zero Room Rent Capping)
Under Cigna Global Health Options (Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers), there is **zero room rent capping**. The patient is entitled to a standard private room with private en-suite bathroom. No proportionate reduction is deducted from surgical suite costs, specialist consultation charges, anesthetist fees, or intensive care charges.

## 6. Medical Evacuation, Repatriation & Organ Transplant
- In the event of emergency medical complications in locations lacking adequate tertiary medical facilities, emergency medical evacuation to the nearest regional center of medical excellence is covered at 100%.
- Medical repatriation back to the home country or primary country of residence is covered when clinically recommended.
- Organ donor hospitalization costs and recipient surgical fees are covered up to policy limits.

## 7. Claim Deficiency Response and Appeal Mechanism
If direct settlement is unavailable or pre-authorization is denied due to clinical inquiry:
1. Pay and claim reimbursement through the Cigna Close Care / Cigna Global online member portal or mobile app within 90 days of treatment completion.
2. Submit official itemized billing invoices, medical receipts, itemized pharmacy bills, diagnostic reports, and treating physician's clinical summary.
3. If an initial claim is rejected, an appeal may be filed within 180 days with supplementary medical records. Formal disputes can be escalated to the Cigna Global Clinical Appeals Board.

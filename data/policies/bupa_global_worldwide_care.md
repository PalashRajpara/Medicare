# Bupa Global Worldwide Medical Insurance Guidelines

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
Treatment for general illness and medical symptoms is eligible following an initial 30-day waiting period from policy commencement. Immediate worldwide coverage applies without waiting periods for emergency hospitalization resulting from accidental bodily injury.

## 2. Specified Illnesses & Joint Surgeries Waiting Period
A mandatory 24-month waiting period applies for elective degenerative joint and skeletal reconstructive surgeries:
- Total knee arthroplasty (TKA), unicompartmental knee replacement, and total hip arthroplasty (THA).
- Spinal decompression, discectomy, and degenerative scoliosis surgeries.
- Chronic benign conditions including gallstones, renal calculi, varicose veins, and benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH).
- Cataract extraction and intraocular lens implantation.

## 3. Pre-Existing Conditions Waiting Period
Under standard underwriting terms, pre-existing conditions disclosed and accepted at enrollment require 24 months of continuous renewal before claims can be authorized. If enrolled under a 24-month moratorium clause, conditions are eligible only after a continuous 24-month period elapsed without any medication, consultation, diagnostic tests, or medical symptoms.

## 4. Cashless Direct Settlement Protocol
- **Direct Settlement Network**: Bupa Global operates an extensive direct billing network with premier healthcare providers throughout India and globally, including Apollo Hospitals, Fortis Hospitals, Medanta The Medicity, Tata Medical Center, Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, and Manipal Hospitals.
- **Pre-Authorization Requirement**: Planned hospitalizations must be notified at least 5 business days prior to scheduled admission. Emergency admissions must be notified within 48 hours of admission.
- Upon clinical verification, Bupa Global issues a direct Guarantee of Payment (GOP) directly to the medical provider in Indian Rupees (INR) or preferred convertible currency.

## 5. Room Rent Eligibility & Hospital Charges
Bupa Global Worldwide plans provide comprehensive in-patient accommodation covering a **Deluxe Single Private Room** without any room rent sub-limits or co-payments. Hospital daily charges, nursing care, surgeon fees, anesthesiologist fees, ICU monitoring, and operating theater suites are settled on an actuals basis without proportionate deduction penalties.

## 6. Advanced Therapeutics & Cancer Care
- Comprehensive in-patient and day-patient oncology treatment including chemotherapy, targeted biological therapy, immunotherapy, and tomotherapy are covered up to the annual aggregate limit.
- Robotic-assisted surgery and minimally invasive endoscopic procedures are covered on an actual incurred basis when clinically certified.

## 7. Claim Deficiency Response and Appeal Mechanism
In cases where cashless GOP is not pre-arranged:
1. Pay the hospital upon discharge and submit a Reimbursement Claim via the Bupa Global MembersWorld portal within 120 days.
2. Required documents: fully itemized hospital discharge summary, original invoices, proof of payment, diagnostic laboratory and radiology results, and doctor's operative notes.
3. In case of dispute or denial, submit a clinical redetermination request within 90 days. Further grievance reviews can be escalated to the Bupa Global Customer Relations and Ombudsman Service.

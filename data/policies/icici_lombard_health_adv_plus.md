# ICICI Lombard Health AdvantEdge & Complete Health Policy

## 1. Initial Waiting Period
Hospitalization claims within the first 30 days of the policy start date are inadmissible, except for accidental bodily injury needing immediate emergency in-patient admission.

## 2. Specific Illnesses Waiting Period (24 Months)
A 24-month continuous renewal waiting period applies for:
- Knee replacement, hip replacement, and degenerative joint disorders.
- Cataract surgery (sub-limits specified per tier: up to ₹40,000 per eye).
- Gallbladder stones, renal stones, and prostate hypertrophy.
- Hysterectomy, prolapsed disc, and varicose veins.

## 3. Pre-Existing Ailment Waiting Period
Declared pre-existing ailments (such as cardiovascular conditions, diabetes, and hypertension) are covered after 36 months of continuous coverage.

## 4. Cashless Hospitalization Protocol
- Supported across ICICI Lombard's 10,000+ empaneled hospitals.
- Planned admission pre-auth must be logged 48 hours prior. Emergency admission requires intimation within 24 hours.
- Direct digital approval via the IL TakeCare portal or hospital TPA counter.

## 5. Room Category & Deductions
No capping on room rent for policies with Sum Insured ₹5,00,000 and above (Single Private AC Room permitted). Proportionate deductions do not apply to medical practitioner fees or OT expenses when staying within the eligible room tier.

## 6. Advanced Procedures & Domiciliary Hospitalization
Includes coverage for robotic surgery, stem cell therapy, and organ donor medical costs. Domiciliary hospitalization is covered if the patient cannot be moved or if local hospital beds are unavailable.

## 7. Dispute Redressal & Ombudsman
Appeals against cashless rejection or claim deductions can be submitted to ICICI Lombard's Grievance Redressal Officer within 45 days. If dissatisfied, the claimant may file an appeal before the relevant regional Insurance Ombudsman.
